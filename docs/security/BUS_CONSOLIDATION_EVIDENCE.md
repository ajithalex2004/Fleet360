# Item 4 — historical route consolidation evidence

The Route Consolidation page now offers **Check 30-day attendance and cost evidence** on a recommendation. The read-only endpoint requires the tenant's Bus Ops admin grant, scopes the routes and all trip/attendance/log reads to the authenticated tenant, and returns aggregates without rider names or IDs. It does not apply a merge.

For each completed trip in the preceding 7–90 days, it reads active manifest rows and the latest trip log. It pairs one trip from each proposed route on the same Dubai service day, shift and direction when scheduled departures are within 60 minutes. Multiple trips on one route in the same service group are ambiguous, so they do not qualify as a one-bus pair. The screen reports completed trips, paired service days, missing attendance, peak reserved and boarded riders, observed capacity conflicts, and log coverage. The assumed capacity is the larger of the two recorded route capacities; it is not proof a suitable vehicle is available. Incomplete or invalid fuel, mileage and time logs are excluded from the observed cost total, with their count disclosed. The rates mirror the existing Bus Ops analytics defaults and environment overrides.

`HISTORICALLY_PLAUSIBLE` only means the observed paired service demand fits that optimistic capacity and manifest rows exist. `CAPACITY_CONFLICT` blocks that inference. `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` includes missing attendance, unknown capacity, no qualifying pair or truncated sampling (more than 5,000 completed trips). This is an evidence aid, not a recommendation to apply automatically. It does not prove stop-level time windows, actual vehicle/driver duty, no-show behavior, contract costs or realized savings. The modeled weekly saving in the analyzer remains an estimate; this endpoint deliberately returns `realizedSavingsAED: null`.

Acceptance on actual customer data remains open because no authorized customer export or staging database connection was available in this workspace. In signed-in staging, run the check for representative high-volume and low-volume route pairs, reconcile attendance rows against manifests and trip logs, inspect ambiguous/headway cases, and compare estimates to a controlled pre/post period after operational approval. Record tenant, date window, route pair IDs, rates, sampling coverage, observed costs, and actual resource assignments in restricted release evidence. Do not include passenger PII in the PR.
